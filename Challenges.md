## Challenges

### Crystal Packing and Band Structure of an Organic Molecular Crystal

Brief: Choose a small semi-conducting organic molecule. Using a known crystal structure (from a CIF file), import it into ASE. Then:
- Optimize the geometry under periodic boundary conditions.
- Compute the electronic band structure and density of states for the crystal.
- Analyse how molecular packing influences the bandgap / DOS.
- Stretch: Compare with a different polymorph or a hypothetical altered packing and see how the bandgap / electronic structure changes.

### Defect Physics and Optoelectronic Properties of an Inorganic Crystal 

Brief: Using a simple inorganic crystalline material (e.g. NaCl, or perhaps a metal halide, or a simple oxide):
- Build the pristine crystal unit cell, expand to a supercell.
- Introduce a point defect: vacancy, interstitial or substitution (e.g. remove an atom, or substitute with a different atomic species).
- Relax the geometry.
- Compute the electronic structure (DOS / band structure) of both pristine and defective crystal, and analyze the changes (appearance of defect states, bandgap narrowing, etc.).
- Stretch: Discuss implications for optical properties (e.g. defect-induced mid-gap states, non-radiative recombination, implications for luminescence or absorption).
